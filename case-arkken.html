The challenge: building a premium brand for optimized for conversion

Arkken's contant marketing team was struggling. They needed to launch new landing pages quickly to support their campaigns and growth hacking initiatives, but their process was slow and cumbersome. Every new page was a custom project, which led to brand inconsistencies and long development cycles.

They also had a lead generation problem. Their forms weren't integrated with their CRM (HubSpot), so leads were falling through the cracks. They needed a system that was fast, consistent, and connected.

The solution: A reusable system for rapid deployment

We built Arkken a Webflow-based system that enabled them to deploy, iterate, and optimize landing pages efficiently while maintaining brand integrity.

First, we developed a set of responsive, visually stunning landing page templates from their Figma designs. We also created a library of reusable components that their marketing team could use to build new pages without writing a single line of code.

Next, we integrated HubSpot with their Webflow forms to capture leads directly into their CRM. We also connected Webflow and Zapier to automate workflows, reducing manual tasks and improving efficiency.

Finally, we built a JavaScript-based configurator to allow users to customize their selections and enhance their engagement with the products or services. This was a key feature that set their landing pages apart from the competition.

The results: Faster, smarter, and more agile

The new system transformed Arkken's marketing efforts. They could now launch new landing pages 40% faster, which allowed them to gather customer feedback more quickly and iterate on their campaigns.

The HubSpot integration led to a 30% increase in leads captured, with automated workflows ensuring no opportunities were missed. The marketing team reported being able to implement updates 3x faster thanks to Webflow's intuitive interface and our reusable components.

The result was a more agile, customer-focused digital presence that drives business growth. Arkken could now move at the speed of their ideas, without sacrificing quality or brand consistency.
