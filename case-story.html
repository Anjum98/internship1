The challenge: A beautiful design that was hard to build

Storylake had a new brand identity and a stunning set of Figma designs to go with it. The problem? The designs were complex, with intricate animations, micro-interactions, and a high level of visual polish. They needed a partner who could translate that vision into a real, functioning website without compromising on the details.

They also needed the site to be multilingual to support their global audience, and they wanted to integrate lead generation to turn their beautiful new site into a business asset. It was a tall order.

The solution: Bringing Figma to life in Webflow

We took on the challenge by building a scalable and engaging Webflow website that brought their vision to life.

First, we translated their Figma designs into a pixel-perfect Webflow implementation. We integrated micro-interactions, Lottie animations, and scroll-triggered animations to create a dynamic and immersive user experience. The site felt alive, just like their brand.

Next, we built a native multilingual site using Webflow's capabilities, enabling Storylake to connect with users in multiple regions. We implemented a seamless language-switching mechanism to enhance usability for international visitors.

Finally, we designed a visually appealing multi-step form and integrated lead magnets to attract and capture new leads. The form was so engaging that it actually improved the user experience instead of feeling like a chore.

The results: A successful rebrand and more business

The new website was a huge success. It perfectly captured Storylake's refreshed brand identity and creative vision. The use of animations and interactions resulted in a 30% improvement in average session duration, and the combination of lead magnets and optimized forms led to a 25% increase in sign-ups.

The multilingual functionality enabled Storylake to effectively reach and engage an international audience, expanding their market presence. The new site not only looked great but also supported their business growth by driving engagement and increasing leads. Interested?
